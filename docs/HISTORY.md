## Historical Rules

Do not confuse the canonical SUGR asset with other tokens using Sugar,
Sugar Bush, or similar names.

The policy ID is the canonical identifier.

Do not publish historical tokenomics, burn totals, market capitalization,
exchange listings, treasury movements or holder statistics as historical
facts until they have been independently verified.

## 2013

The 2013 event was a Bitcoin promotional concept involving Charles
Hoskinson and Sugar Bush.

No SUGR token existed in 2013.

## 2024

The canonical SUGR Cardano asset was created on November 14, 2024.

On-chain records show active DEX trading during November 2024.

## 2025

Sugar Factory staking existed.

The surviving staking interface states that reward claims operated on
10-day intervals from January 8, 2025 through January 6, 2026.

## 2026

A community-led revival of the existing SUGR project began.

The revival does not create a replacement token.

The original Cardano SUGR asset remains the canonical token.
