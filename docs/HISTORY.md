## Historical Rules

Do not confuse the canonical SUGR asset with other tokens using Sugar,
Sugar Bush, or similar names.

The policy ID is the canonical identifier.

Do not publish historical tokenomics, burn totals, market capitalization,
exchange listings, treasury movements or holder statistics as historical
facts until they have been independently verified.

## 2013

The 2013 event was a Bitcoin promotional concept involving Charles
Hoskinson and Sugar Bush.

No SUGR token existed in 2013.

## 2024

The canonical SUGR Cardano asset was created on November 14, 2024.

On-chain records show active DEX trading during November 2024.

## 2025

Sugar Factory staking existed.

The surviving staking interface states that reward claims operated on
10-day intervals from January 8, 2025 through January 6, 2026.

## 2026

A community-led revival of the existing SUGR project began.

The revival does not create a replacement token.

The original Cardano SUGR asset remains the canonical token.

## 2025 Sugar Factory

Status: Existence independently documented

The surviving official Sugar Factory staking interface states that
staking rewards were claimable every 10 days from January 8, 2025
through January 6, 2026.

Detailed Factory economics remain under verification.

## 2025 Market Decline

Status: Third-party historical market data

Available historical market data indicates that SUGR lost the vast
majority of its market value during 2025.

Do not publish an exact decline percentage as canonical history until
multiple historical price sources have been reconciled.

## Legacy Infrastructure Account

Status: First-hand statement from legacy developer; not independently verified

During August 2026 revival discussions, the legacy Factory developer
stated that infrastructure funding originally allocated in SUGR became
insufficient after depreciation in both SUGR and ADA.

He also stated that he had personally continued paying for hosting of
the public sugarcardano.io website.

These statements must be attributed if published and must not be
presented as independently verified facts.

## 2026 Factory Withdrawal Recovery

Status: Revival-team record

The revival team is coordinating with the legacy Factory developer to
restore the Factory temporarily so remaining legacy users can withdraw.

The quoted restoration cost is 200 USDM.

The revival team requested:
- withdrawal access for existing stakers
- deposits/new staking disabled if possible
- a defined withdrawal window
- invoice/evidence for infrastructure spending

Do not publish completion of this process until the Factory has actually
been restored and withdrawal functionality has been verified.
