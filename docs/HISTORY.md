# SUGR Historical Record

This document contains historical facts approved for use on the official
SUGR revival website.

Claims should only be added after verification.

## Evidence Classes

Information in this record may be classified as:

**Verified Fact**  
Supported by primary records, on-chain evidence or independently
verifiable sources.

**Historical Snapshot**  
A dated contemporary statistic or observation. Historical snapshots
must not be presented as current information.

**Attributed Testimony**  
A statement from an identified historical participant that has not
been independently verified.

**Under Verification**  
Information for which evidence exists but is not yet sufficient for
publication as canonical fact.

**Revival-Team Record**  
A current or historical project event documented directly by the
revival team. It should not be represented as independent third-party
verification unless separately corroborated.

## Historical Rules

Do not confuse the canonical SUGR asset with other tokens using Sugar,
Sugar Bush, or similar names.

The policy ID is the canonical identifier.

Do not publish historical tokenomics, burn totals, market capitalization,
exchange listings, treasury movements or holder statistics as historical
facts until they have been independently verified.

## 2013

Status: Verified Fact

The 2013 event was a Bitcoin promotional concept involving Charles
Hoskinson and Sugar Bush.

No SUGR token existed in 2013.

## 2024

The canonical SUGR Cardano asset was created on November 14, 2024.

On-chain records show active DEX trading during November 2024.

## 2025

Historical evidence confirms that Sugar Factory staking existed.

See "2025 Sugar Factory" below for the surviving documented
staking-interface evidence.

Available historical market data also indicates that SUGR lost the
vast majority of its market value during 2025.

See "2025 Market Decline" below for evidence classification.

## 2025 Sugar Factory

Status: Verified Fact

The surviving official Sugar Factory staking interface states that
staking rewards were claimable every 10 days from January 8, 2025
through January 6, 2026.

Detailed Factory economics remain under verification.

Do not publish specific withdrawal fees, redistribution percentages,
burn percentages, reward amounts or other Factory mechanics as
canonical facts until independently verified.

## 2025 Market Decline

Status: Verified Fact
Evidence: Historical market data from independently reviewable third-party sources

Available historical market data establishes that SUGR lost the vast
majority of its market value during 2025.

The exact magnitude remains under verification.

Do not publish an exact decline percentage as canonical history until
multiple historical price sources have been reconciled.

## Attributed Legacy Developer Testimony — Infrastructure

Status: Attributed Testimony
Source: Legacy Factory developer
Context: August 2026 revival discussions

During August 2026 revival discussions, the legacy Factory developer
stated that infrastructure funding originally allocated in SUGR became
insufficient after depreciation in both SUGR and ADA.

He also stated that he had personally continued paying for hosting of
the public sugarcardano.io website.

These statements must be attributed if published and must not be
presented as independently verified facts.
