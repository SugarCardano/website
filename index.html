Charles Hoskinson

Sugar Bush the Squirrel

In 2013, Charles Hoskinson had a hair brained idea: enlist Sugar Bush, the world's most photographed squirrel, to promote Bitcoin - a move so ahead of its time it predated the creation of meme coins.

Sadly, the world wasn’t ready for such genius, and the idea was "berried" on Bitcoin forums, forgotten for years...

Until finally in 2024, the story resurfaced and inspired the creation of $SUGR, a meme coin that brings Charles' vision to life, but this time on Cardano, the blockchain he founded.

With Sugar Bush’s irresistible charm, $SUGR community's purpose is to make blockchain fun and accessible for everyone.
